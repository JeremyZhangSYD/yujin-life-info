HOW THE PRIVATE ASSISTANT WORKS

From a question to a useful first pass.

  1. 01

    Search with a purpose

    The owner asks a question. The assistant searches a bounded date or topic range through the official Gmail API.

  2. 02

    Read relevant evidence

    It retrieves message metadata, snippets, and selected plain-text bodies. Attachment downloads and HTML rendering are not supported.

  3. 03

    Summarize, not execute

    The configured AI model helps produce summaries and possible tasks. Source IDs and coverage limits keep the result checkable.

A CLEARER EXPECTATION

Helpful context.
Human decisions.

Email excerpts needed for a requested summary may be sent to the owner's configured AI processing provider. Read-only describes Gmail permissions; it does not mean all processing stays on the device.

Searches may be partial, and AI suggestions can be wrong. Verify deadlines, amounts, and commitments against the original message. No payment or external commitment is made by this Gmail integration.
