1. Purpose and availability

Yujin Life Gmail is a personal-use project maintained by JeremyZhangSYD. Its Gmail features operate in an owner-controlled private workspace. This website provides public application information and policy notices; it does not provide accounts, access to a mailbox, or a hosted assistant for visitors.

There is no subscription, payment, or purchase flow on this documentation site. Publication of these pages is not an invitation to use the owner's private application or to connect another person's Google account.

2. Authorized, read-only access

The Gmail integration is limited to the configured owner and the Google account they authorize. Use it only for mail you are authorized to access and only for lawful purposes. Do not attempt to bypass identity, permission, or security controls.

The current integration reads supported Gmail data. It does not send mail, change mailbox state, download attachments, or execute instructions found inside a message. Email content is evidence to be interpreted, not authority to change the application's rules.

3. Verify important information

Searches are bounded and may omit relevant messages. Message snippets or plain-text bodies may be incomplete, and the assistant may misunderstand a sender, date, amount, or requested action. A possible task is not necessarily an obligation, and a marketing invitation is not a confirmed commitment.

Review source messages before relying on a summary, replying, paying a bill, attending an event, or making a decision. Suggested text is a draft, not a sent message. The assistant's output is not professional financial, legal, medical, or other regulated advice.

4. No automatic commitments or continuity promise

The Gmail integration does not make payments, reservations, contractual commitments, or other external changes. Those actions remain with the owner outside this read-only integration.

The project is provided on a personal, evolving basis without a service-level or uninterrupted-availability promise. Google authorization, API access and independent AI services can expire, fail or change. A production OAuth publishing status does not mean a grant can never expire or be revoked.

5. Privacy, changes, and contact

The privacy policy explains Gmail access, AI processing, storage, retention and disconnection. Gmail data may be processed by the AI inference provider configured by the owner to provide requested summaries. Read-only permissions do not mean processing is entirely local.

The maintainer may update these notices as the personal application evolves. Material permission or data-use changes require appropriate disclosure and authorization before activation.

For general questions, use the public issue tracker. Do not submit mail contents, credentials, or sensitive details. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or represented as verified by Google.
